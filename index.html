Controls

Touch
Tap to open. Long-press to flag. Tap a number whose flags are all placed to open the rest around it. Flag mode swaps tap and long-press.
Mouse
Left click opens, right click flags. Click a satisfied number, press both buttons or the middle button to clear around it.
Keys
Arrows move, Space opens, F flags, H shows a hint, D the details after a game, S the statistics, N starts a new game.
Replay
Space plays or pauses, ← → step a move, Home End jump to the start or the end, Escape closes.
Hint
Marks a cell that is safe for sure, or the safest guess when there is none. Not in ranked games. A win with a hint keeps no time and counts as played, not won.
Daily
One no-guess board a day for each level, the same for everyone, with the same opening. Only your first try counts; later ones are practice. Days change at midnight in Oslo.
